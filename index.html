About Zyqor Intern

Zyqor Intern is an academic startup concept exploring how short-term project-based work could structurally fix the entry-level experience paradox.

All projects, figures, and UI elements presented on this page are illustrative prototypes designed to demonstrate the platform's potential value proposition to both students and small businesses.
