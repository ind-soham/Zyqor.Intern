Frequently Asked Questions

What is a micro-internship?
A short-term, paid, professional assignment that is similar to those given to new hires or interns, typically lasting a few days to a few weeks.
Are projects paid?
Yes. All projects on MicroIntern are paid. Companies set a fixed budget for the project deliverables.
